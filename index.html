About the series

Barely Built follows three friends in their mid-20s who start a small business from an apartment. They thrift clothes, restyle them and sell them online.

It's a comedy about being broke, figuring out adult life and building something of their own, told in a bright, cinematic 3D animated style.

Every episode happens in Anna's apartment: terracotta walls, string lights, sticky notes everywhere, and a clothing rack that's always one hanger away from collapsing.

Barely Built is an original series by Pete's Creates. New episodes go up on Instagram at @petes_creates.

Client: Celiac Nourrition · @celiac.nourrition

Celiac Nourrition

I made this 50-second animated ad for a nutrition account for people with celiac disease.

It plays out in the Barely Built kitchen: a worried Anna, a toaster full of crumbs, and a dietitian who walks in to explain what is safe to eat. My goal was to turn a dry health topic into a short story people want to watch and share.

I made it the same way I make the series: AI-animated 3D characters, a script written for the topic, and a vertical cut made for Instagram. Tap play (sound on) to watch.
